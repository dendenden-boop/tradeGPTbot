## Contract

`calculatePaperInventoryValuation` is a pure calculation exported by the PAPER root. Its strict input contains the current complete PAPER inventory, explicit evaluation time/maximum age, common valuation currency and direct scoped Spot LAST prices. Its immutable `PAPER_INVENTORY_VALUATION_CALCULATION` contains per-account/per-asset values, total equity and an exclusive mathematical freshness deadline. It contains no certificate, grant, decision, reservation or completeness attestation.

The generic Portfolio view correctly considers an old native snapshot stale. PAPER capture instead proves that retained virtual balances have not changed since the original seed and separately captures current ownership/catalog/draft state. The calculation preserves that original `snapshotAt` and values the retained balances at the explicit current prices. It never rewrites a Portfolio snapshot or passes historical state into native certification as renewed evidence.

All inventory accounts must be present once, sorted and scoped to one tenant/PAPER owner. Every account must retain its original funding/configuration/book identity and identical pristine common state. Current balance changes, positions, pending activity or holds reject wholly. The accepted SQL reader remains responsible for current ownership, complete catalog, immutable seals and absence of active financial activity; this calculation does not independently attest any of those facts. Hash-shaped values, caller objects and JSON roundtrips cannot prove provenance or authorize money. The future coordinator must recapture real inventory and market heads in its own bounded transaction.

Every funded asset outside the valuation currency requires exactly one account-scoped direct LAST price in the account's exact public source exchange/market/region/environment. The same public source/asset/currency across accounts must bind identical price, time and source identity. Missing, duplicate, conflicting, foreign, future, stale, unused or unknown-field evidence fails closed. The valuation currency uses identity one without a fabricated market price. No multi-hop FX, stablecoin equivalence, unsupported asset skipping or partial estimate is inferred. All account configurations must use the requested valuation currency.

At most 100 accounts, 1000 aggregate CREATED drafts, 3200 prices and 1 MiB encoded calculation input. Bounds reject the whole calculation. Existing exact Decimal operations keep monetary values as strings, including quantities of 1e-18 and balances above 2^53. Results outside 30 integer/18 fractional digits fail closed; no rounding, clipping or silent top-up. No Number monetary conversion or process-wide Decimal configuration is used. The exclusive deadline includes the current inventory and every price; reaching it invalidates the calculation without renewing original snapshot times.
