# PAPER commitment projection

Starting protected main: `619ff1a0c0837af2f05e15b999fa619bc52dc426`. The thirteen-command baseline passed before tracked changes. This is a pure content contract needed before an atomic PAPER reservation writer; it does not implement that writer or make non-pristine accounts admissible.

## Projection contract

`projectPaperRiskCommitments` in the pure `@ctp/paper-engine` root accepts one account's current common Portfolio state, bigint-safe book revision, original capture time, valuation currency, reservation allocations and latest hold events/watermarks. It returns sorted immutable account-local balances, reservations and holds compatible with the existing conservative vector budget. No I/O, reduction, financial posting, certificate, grant, permit or dispatch occurs. No `complete` assertion is returned.

Each allocation permanently identifies its reservation/order/intent, instrument and base/quote, side, valuation notional/currency and the entire positive debit vector. IDs, order/intent correlation, allocated assets, hold IDs and last-event identities cannot conflict or be shared. A BUY allocates quote; a SELL allocates base and any actually allocated quote commission. A proved zero-commission SELL may have only base. This projection checks consistency of supplied allocations; it does not independently reprice or authenticate their original model bounds. The future coordinator must bind them to immutable durable decisions and current rules/model evidence.

ACTIVE and UNKNOWN require every allocated live hold exactly once, with equal asset/amount, the account-local Portfolio state, matching latest event fingerprint/timestamp and matching watermark flags. UNKNOWN cannot be projected from RESERVED/PENDING content, nor ACTIVE from UNKNOWN content. All current holds must be represented; omitted, shared, orphan, conflicting or future content rejects. Notionals remain one liability per reservation; the budget subsequently correlates dispatched orders by order/intent and takes the conservative maximum rather than adding them again.

RELEASED allocations are omitted from the result only if every allocated hold is absent and has a matching RELEASE event and released watermark. The event must describe definitive resolution (`resolved=true`). That flag and fingerprint merely validate content: they cannot authorize a release, prove its economic outcome, or replace the existing trusted resolution boundary. Latest RESOLVE_COMMITMENT content must match the persisted RESERVED hold and watermark; the projection cannot manufacture or authenticate the resolution proof. Shared Portfolio tests exercise the actual reducer's ordering/fingerprint rules, including stale replay after release.

PAPER cash is gross ledger cash: total/free/available agree, locked is zero, and all commitment holds are unreflected. Aggregate held amounts must fit account-local cash. No native collateral credit, account pooling, anticipated proceeds or second monetary ledger effect is introduced. The common budget deducts each projected hold once. Native accounting/adapters are unchanged.

The original capture `asOf` supplies an exclusive bounded deadline; the age bound is 1–5000 ms. Persisted old UNKNOWN holds do not expire because their economic event is old. Snapshot/economic timestamps are preserved and cannot be future; GAP, differences, missing snapshot and unresolved Portfolio pending entries reject. SPOT/PAPER/null private connection/exact owner and bootstrap wallet are required. Public market source environment may remain LIVE, as before; this is not financial LIVE trading. Decimal amounts and signed-bigint-range revision strings never round through Number. Arrays and serialized content are bounded; excess rejects without eviction. Output ordering uses ordinal string comparison across OSes.

## Authority and persistence boundary

A self-consistent JSON capture, latest-event fingerprint or successful projection proves no current SQL source, completeness, history authenticity or resolution authority. The accepted pristine-only source and Risk calculation still reject this projection as a replacement source. Financial SQL admission and Order Engine dispatch remain closed for PAPER. The function does not certify positions, current mark/FX/liquidity, general loss history, policy/permission epochs or operational health.

Before any writer, the fixed SQL coordinator must capture the complete current tenant/account inventory under the shared lock order; authenticate immutable vectors, decisions and definitive outcomes; retain durable monotone watermarks/tombstones and permanent idempotency; enforce budgets atomically with Portfolio COMMITMENT; treat uncertain COMMIT as no grant; and perform final dispatch validation. It must not delete history to permit identifier reuse, trust caller-supplied completeness, default unknown liabilities to empty, or grant from this result. Historical released rows supplied here are checked against their tombstones; this bounded projection is not an all-history persistence store. A future persistent selection must prove complete active inventory and preserve all durable lifecycle identities independently of this input bound.
