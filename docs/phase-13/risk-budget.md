This increment starts from protected main **361770e5b2c3b58cf33c88b50310d9f0ed839972**. `calculatePaperRiskBudget`, exported by the pure `@ctp/paper-engine` root, extends the accepted lifetime envelope and multi-asset coverage arithmetic with aggregate exposure limits. ## Inputs and accounting

The strict input contains the full original command, model, current instrument/rules and public book/trade; PAPER owner and new order/intent identifiers; platform/user limits; quote-to-valuation FX; and one complete normalized inventory. The inventory contains up to 100 accounts, 30,000 Spot positions, 10,000 pending orders, 10,000 reservations with at most two positive debits each, 20,000 holds and 32 unique asset availability amounts per account. Unknown fields, non-PAPER scope, absent target account, contradictory currency/instrument mappings, duplicate identities, incomplete or foreign inventory and negative Spot positions reject.

The future server must derive the owner from authentication and prove the entire inventory in one bounded transaction. UUIDs, supplied policies, `complete: true`, serialized rows and the result establish no ownership or source authority. The existing pristine PAPER source readers cannot supply active reservation/hold history; they remain unchanged and continue to reject it. Independently reading those ports and joining their objects does not certify this input.

Each pending order must match exactly one reservation by order, intent, account, instrument, base/quote and side. A reservation before dispatch may exist without an order projection and still counts. Correlated order/reservation exposure is their larger valuation notional, counted once. Portfolio holds are collateral, never additional exposure. Each positive reservation debit must match its own exact account/asset/amount hold and availability row. BUY retains quote collateral; SELL retains base principal plus any separately funded quote commission. Orphan/shared/missing/conflicting holds or vectors reject the whole calculation.

Bounded UNKNOWN remains counted and fully held. An UNKNOWN order requires an UNKNOWN reservation; that reservation requires UNKNOWN holds for every debit. Unknown exposure without a positive bound rejects. Only unreflected holds reduce the supplied availability; reflected holds have already reduced it and are not deducted twice. This flag requires durable Portfolio proof in the unfinished coordinator and grants no positive native collateral credit. No release, tombstone, event ordering or persistence is implemented by this calculator.

Current position exposure reuses common Risk position/FX arithmetic, with one representation per account/instrument and account/base Spot holding. Instrument and base-asset budgets are local to the target account, account exposure includes its other instruments, and user exposure includes all supplied tenant accounts. Pending orders/reservations add conservatively for both BUY and SELL; expected sales never net down holdings or fund commissions. Potential positions use the union of actual holdings and pending instruments, so a pending entry without a fill still occupies its slot. The candidate new intent cannot already appear in the inventory; authoritative permanent replay lookup remains a durable gateway responsibility.

## Limits and freshness

The platform/user intersection uses the existing common Risk limits contract. The calculator enforces max order notional, instrument/asset/account/user exposure, open orders and potential positions. Exact equality passes; one atom above a limit rejects. It adds the recomputed lifetime quote notional, converted to valuation currency, to each aggregate budget. Money and FX use checked canonical decimal strings, never monetary JS Number conversions, implicit currency pooling or iteration proportional to order lots. Collateral remains in its original assets.

Identity FX requires the same currency and exact rate one. Observed FX requires the exact quote-to-valuation pair, freshness and consistent rates for the same pair across positions. Conflicting observed rates reject rather than select a convenient value. Overflow or excess precision fails closed.

The stricter policy/model age applies to inventory, FX, every position mark/FX and every book/trade receipt/native timestamp. The exclusive result deadline includes the earliest of these witnesses and the accepted envelope/rules expiry; the original model and historical observation times are never rewritten. Tighter policy does not renew older market evidence.

This result does **not** evaluate loss baselines, permissions, capabilities, controls, circuits, health, order frequency, minimum liquidity, spread, price deviation, leverage or minimum remaining valuation balance. These checks, current policy/rule/permission revisions, source certification, atomic vector reservation/Portfolio commitment, UNKNOWN/release ordering and final dispatch validation remain mandatory at the future financial boundary. Passing these budget checks is not full Risk approval.
